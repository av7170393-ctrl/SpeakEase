How SpeakEase works

  • Pick a level – A1–A2, B1–B2 or C1–C2.
  • Speak or type – the mic turns speech into text (Chrome works best).
  • Get kind feedback – strengths first, gentle tips, a polished answer, pronunciation help and a grammar buddy.
  • Track growth – watch your progress chart.

Interview tip: the STAR method

Situation → Task → Action → Result. Use it for "Tell me about a challenge" questions.

About the project

Built by Aryan Verma (B.Tech CSE – AI & ML). Tech: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and the Web Speech API. Scoring and feedback are rule-based. A Python Flask + SQLite backend version is also available.
